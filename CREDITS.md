# Credits

GoldLink stands on the work of many people. As hg-engine asks, its credits are replicated here.

## GoldLink

- eloyball and the GoldLink contributors: the six-player co-op (ROM code, Co-op Bridge, relay,
  launcher).

GoldLink is built on hg-engine by BluRosie and its contributors. hg-engine's own credits follow,
as its README and `CREDITS.md` give them.

* Rafael Vuijk (ndstool)
